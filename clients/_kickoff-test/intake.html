Agent Readiness Roadmap · Kickoff Intake

The facts only you hold

We build your Roadmap files from your public website — the crawl, the extraction, the schema, all of it. This short form asks only for what no crawler can find: the numbers, dates, licenses and identifiers that live inside your business, not on your site.

Why this is short by design. Everything we can read for ourselves, we do. If a field feels like something an AI agent could already find on your site, it isn’t here. That’s the point — each blank below is also a readiness gap, because a buyer’s AI agent hits the same wall we did.

1

Certifications & standards

A buyer’s agent verifies a certificate by its number against the issuing registry — the type alone (“ISO 9001”) proves nothing a machine can check. Dates and facility scope stop a stale or mismatched claim from being propagated as fact.

2

Professional & engineering licenses

Optional

License numbers and states are almost never published — but they’re exactly what a vetting agent needs to confirm a real, current credential behind your engineering claims.

3

Product identifiers & unpublished specs

MPN and SKU are how a procurement agent keys your product across catalogs and maps an inquiry back to your own systems. Publishing spec min/max as facts (not a buried PDF table) is what lets an agent filter you into a shortlist.

4

Testimonial & case attribution

Optional

An agent weighs attributed proof and discards the anonymous. “J. from Ohio” carries no verifiable weight; a named, titled reference does. List any first-name-only or anonymous testimonials you can upgrade.

5

Actions endpoints

Optional

Only if you add the Actions module later. These declare a machine-facing quote and catalog surface in /.well-known/. Supplying them now commits to nothing and activates nothing — the hooks ship dormant.
