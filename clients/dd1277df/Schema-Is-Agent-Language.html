Schema Priorities for B2B Distribution

Organization — full alternateName, legalName, sameAs network, foundingDate, founder, knowsAbout
LocalBusiness — on every location page
Product / Offer — on every catalog page (most distributors have NONE)
BreadcrumbList — site-wide
Service — on service pages
Article + author — on case studies, blog, educational content

What This Means For Your Site

  • Schema's audience is no longer the SERP skimmer. It's the AI engine recommending you (or not) to your buyer.
  • If Organization is missing alternateName, sameAs, foundingDate, knowsAbout — AI engines can't reconcile your brand identity across the web.
  • If product pages have no Product / Offer schema — AI engines can't extract pricing, availability, specs.
  • If service pages have no Service schema — AI engines can't tell what you do vs. what your competitors do.
  • If case studies have no Article + author — your proof signals are invisible.
  • Schema is the disclosure layer that determines whether AI surfaces your brand or your competitor's.
