What's in the package

Nine drop-in files

Each file is tagged by the agent-readiness layer it strengthens: Find, Understand, Use (can an agent act?), and Trust.

Use
quote-request.schema.json
The centerpiece. A machine-readable contract of exactly what you need to return a quote—derived from your existing form. Lets an agent compose a complete, valid request.
Understand
capability-manifest.jsonld
Your “what we can build and quote” envelope: kVA range, voltages, windings, lead-time tiers, services. Puts you in the agent's consideration set.
Understand
product-….jsonld
A structured record per spec page—real attributes plus a “request a quote” action. ~3 readable facts become ~15.
Use
.well-known/api-catalog & agent-quote.json
Discovery signposts that let an agent find your contract and capability. (The agent endpoint is documented as proposed; your human form stays the working fallback.)
Trust
reviews.jsonld
Your real testimonials, marked up as machine-readable trust signals. No fabricated star ratings.
Find
llms.txt
A content map pointing AI crawlers to your most important pages.
