Executive Summary

The Opportunity

AI systems are becoming a primary way potential clients discover and evaluate professional services firms. This roadmap provides Jabian Consulting with a template-by-template implementation plan to ensure AI platforms can accurately find, understand, and recommend your services.

Current State

jabian.com currently has no entity connections visible to AI systems. Across 8 template types covering approximately 32 pages, we identified 13 schema types that need to be added, and content queryability averages 24%.

Recommended Approach

Based on the site's template complexity and page count, we recommend the Standard implementation tier. This covers all 8 template clusters with structured data, semantic HTML improvements, and content optimization guidance.

Expected Outcome

Building these connections from scratch means every improvement directly increases AI comprehension. After implementation, AI platforms will be able to accurately describe your services, connect your team to their expertise, and cite your thought leadership when answering relevant queries.
