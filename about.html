For nearly three decades, I've been the person companies call when the structure of the website itself is the problem.

Not the copy. Not the campaign. The foundation underneath.

  • Domain migrations
  • Platform moves
  • Content restructuring to match a business pivot
  • CMS overhauls that touch every URL
  • International expansions and company divestitures

I've cleaned out the attic and the basement of websites for enterprise B2B companies, global SaaS platforms, insurance groups, and lead generation networks.

Every one of those projects had the same shape: something fundamental about the website has to change, and if you get the structure wrong, everything built on top of it breaks.

Why the agentic web is different

Now the entire web is facing the biggest structural shift I've seen in my career. AI agents have become a primary surface for discovery. They read websites differently than people do, and most websites were never designed to be read that way in the first place.

The companies that figure out how to communicate clearly, completely, and consistently to AI agents will own the next decade of discovery. The ones that don't will watch their visibility erode and never quite know why.

I'm here to help clients lay the foundations they'll need to survive and thrive in this new agentic web. No one can know yet what it will fully become. But I couldn't be more excited to be part of the transition.
