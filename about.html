Why the agentic web is different

Now the entire web is facing the biggest structural shift I've seen in my career. AI agents have become a primary surface for discovery. They read websites differently than people do, and most websites were never designed to be read that way in the first place.

The companies that figure out how to communicate clearly, completely, and consistently to AI agents will own the next decade of discovery. The ones that don't will watch their visibility erode and never quite know why.

I'm here to help clients lay the foundations they'll need to survive and thrive in this new agentic web. No one can know yet what it will fully become. But I couldn't be more excited to be part of the transition.

The Find / Understand / Trust framework

I use a three-layer framework to diagnose websites for the agentic web. Each layer asks a different question, and each one can stop an AI agent cold on its own.

Find — Can AI access your site?

The infrastructure layer: robots.txt directives, CDN bot rules, JavaScript rendering, structured data access. If AI can't read your site, nothing else matters.

Read the FIND layer →

Understand — Can AI parse what you do?

The content layer: entity clarity, semantic HTML, schema markup, fact-over-vibe content. If AI can read you but can't interpret you, it fills the gaps with assumptions.

Read the UNDERSTAND layer →

Trust — Will AI cite you over your competitors?

The authority layer: consistent entity signals across the open web, corroborating sources, fresh and definitive content. If AI doesn't trust you, it cites someone else.
