The Four Questions

Do you perform well across Google and AI?

Before any search engine ranks you, any AI answer cites you, or any agent interacts with you, your site has to clear four levels of usability. They're the same four whether the visitor is a person, Google's crawler, or an autonomous agent:

Found
Can they reach you, or are you blocking crawlers and agents by accident?
Understood
Is your content built so a machine can interpret it, not just display it?
Trusted
When an AI decides who to cite or recommend, do the signals point to you?
Usable
Can an agent actually do business with you, or only describe you?

Not every company needs to act on all four areas today. The Review distinguishes what matters now from what can wait.

Also in the Executive Brief, you'll find your three highest impact moves. Open any one to see the finding and the fix.

Critical
You're indexed under two different company names
What we found A modern brand name on newer platforms and a legacy legal name on older B2B directories. With no schema bridge declaring them one company, training-weighted engines treat them as two businesses — which is most of why one engine cites you at 75% and another at 0%.
Recommendation: Add an Organization schema bridge (alternateName + sameAs) and reconcile the legacy directory listings so every engine resolves you to a single entity.
Critical
Your specs and services aren't machine-readable
What we found Product and service details exist only as prose, with generic WebPage schema site-wide. An agent building a comparison represents you with roughly 3 attributes and a competitor with 20 — a data-extractability gap, not a product gap.
Technical detail

Every sampled page emits only WordPress/Yoast defaults — no Product, Service, Organization, or Review types. Requires custom schema templates per page type.

Recommendation: Implement schema.org/Product and schema.org/Service with name, description, provider, areaServed, and offer/spec properties, plus Review schema on testimonials.
Critical
An agent can describe you, but can't do business with you
What we found Your quote-request path is genuinely good for a human, but it's closed to agents by a challenge question and exposes no machine-readable contract. When a buyer's agent is ready to request a quote, it routes to a competitor that published the right surfaces.
Recommendation: Publish a machine-readable "inputs-to-quote" contract and agent-actionable surfaces at /.well-known/, and link product pages to a structured RFQ path with spec context carried through.
